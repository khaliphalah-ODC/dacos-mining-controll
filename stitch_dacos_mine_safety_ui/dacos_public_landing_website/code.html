MISSION STATEMENT & CONTEXT

Advancing worker health & telemetry across West Africa’s extraction corridors.

Industrial mining environments in Liberia—from deep pit iron-ore corridors to underground shaft operations—confront intense humidity, complex geology, and infrastructure friction. DACOS was conceived to deliver high-fidelity digital telemetry and situational awareness where standard consumer networks cannot penetrate.

By integrating simulated mesh communication arrays, low-power edge processing nodes, and an uncompromising operator SCADA interface, DACOS bridges subterranean worker crews directly with surface control rooms to detect critical hazard exposures early.
